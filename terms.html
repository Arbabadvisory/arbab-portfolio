03 The savings calculator

The calculator on this site estimates what your bookkeeping may currently be costing you, and compares that with our published plan prices. It is a conversation starter. It is not a quotation, a valuation, a financial projection, or advice of any kind.

You should understand how it arrives at its figures:

  • The inputs are yours. The hours, the value you place on an hour of your time, and anything you already pay are all figures you supply. We cannot verify them, and the output is only ever as sound as what goes in.
  • The cost of your own time is not money leaving your account. It is the value of hours you could have spent otherwise. Whether that value is real for your business depends entirely on what you would actually have done with the time.
  • The bookkeeper comparison is a model, not a quote. It applies assumed hourly rates and a minimum engagement to your stated workload. Real bookkeepers price differently, and what someone would actually charge you may be higher or lower.
  • Penalties are shown separately and deliberately. They are published figures for filing late. They represent a risk you carry, not a cost you incur every year, and they are not added into any comparison. They are correct to the best of our knowledge on the date shown, but penalty regimes change — always check the current position with the relevant authority.
  • Every assumption is on the page. The "How this is worked out" panel beneath the calculator shows each figure and each sum. Nothing is hidden.

No result the calculator produces is a promise, guarantee or representation that you will save any particular amount, or anything at all. Your actual position depends on your circumstances, and we will only give you a real figure after a proper conversation.
